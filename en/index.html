FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How does the free plan work?

You get 10 videos at start — one time. The bot makes 2 Shorts from each video. Clips carry a watermark. No card needed.

Do I need to edit anything?

No. The bot finds the moments, reframes the video to 9:16 and adds subtitles. You just pick a style and publish.

Which subtitle styles are available?

Four styles: Classic, TikTok, Podcast and Neon. Subtitles are word-by-word — words highlight in sync with speech. Switch with the “Another style” button.

Can I upload my own files?

Yes. Send a video link (up to 60 minutes) or upload a file up to 1.5 GB right in the bot chat.

How is KoShorts different from Opus Clip?

KoShorts lives right in Telegram — no web editor to open or configure. You can pay in UAH or Stars, and there is a free start with no card. It is built by a practicing YouTube creator for real creator workflows.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Cancel in one click inside the bot — no calls or emails. The paid period stays active until the end of its 30 days.
